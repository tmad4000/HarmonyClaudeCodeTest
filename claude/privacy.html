Harmony beta diagnostics

This stable diagnostics build records microphone audio, app sound and technical readings locally when you press Sing. Automatic upload is disabled in this release. Nothing is sent to Jacob unless you export and share a recording yourself.

Turn recording off

Click Recording in the top corner once to stop debug capture. The choice is remembered in this browser. You can keep singing with recording off. Turning it off does not delete recordings already saved.

Export or delete

Use Export debug session below Sing to download the latest saved audio and logs. For large recordings, use the separate Audio and Telemetry buttons under Settings → Debug recording → Saved sessions. Delete removes the selected session from this browser.

Technical readings

We record a random browser install ID, browser and screen details, raw microphone levels and zero runs, track and AudioContext states, sample rate, latency, route labels when available, app settings and version, pitch and gate decisions. This helps distinguish a microphone interruption from the harmony gate closing. We do not use canvas, fonts or other invasive fingerprinting. Feedback loads World Issue Tracker only when you tap Suggestion box.

Storage

Unkept debug sessions expire after 14 days or a 100 MB cap, oldest first. Kept sessions are never automatically deleted. Audio is capped at 10 minutes and 20 MB per session. Anyone using this browser profile can access its local recordings. Browser storage may be cleared by the browser or device, so Export recordings you want to keep.

Public beta upload is planned with a clear notice, a Recording off control, private access, deletion and retention controls. It will be enabled only after a working approved HTTPS route is verified.
