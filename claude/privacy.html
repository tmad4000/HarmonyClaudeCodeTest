Harmony beta recording

Harmony is in beta. Unless you turn Recording off, each session’s microphone audio, the app’s sound and technical readings are sent to Jacob Cole to find and fix audio problems. This starts only after you press Sing and allow microphone access. If other people can be heard where you sing, their voices are recorded too.

Turn recording off

Click Recording in the top corner once. This stops capture and upload, discards pending uploads, and remembers your choice in this browser. You can keep singing with recording off. It does not delete sessions already sent. Turning it on again does not upload old local-only sessions or sessions captured while it was off.

Private debugging, no account needed

Recordings are sent to globalbr.ai and stored in Jacob’s private Noos storage for Jacob and those working on Harmony with him, including AI assistants. They are never published. No account or sign-in is required, and no profile name or email is added. Audio and device labels may themselves contain names. A random browser ID and a private write-and-delete token connect sessions from this browser; they do not grant anyone access to hear recordings.

What is sent

Microphone audio, app audio and technical readings: timing, pitch tracking, gate decisions, echo removal, errors, browser and device type, screen size, microphone and audio-route labels when available, sample rate, latency, track and AudioContext states, app settings, version and build. We do not use canvas, fonts or other invasive fingerprinting. The Suggestion box loads World Issue Tracker only when you tap it.

Retention and limits

After 30 days a recording can no longer be opened or listened to, and its stored file is removed. Backup and recovery copies kept by the storage system may last longer; we cannot yet say how long. This applies to all server debug recordings, including sessions marked Keep. Each upload is capped at 2 MiB and a session at 30 MiB; rate and total storage limits also apply.

Offline upload copies stay in this browser for up to 30 days, with a 100 MiB and 200-session cap. Safari and other browsers may pause uploads when a tab closes; sending resumes when the page is open and online. If sending fails or reaches a limit, the indicator shows it and the local Export remains available.

Export or delete

Use Export debug session below Sing for a local audio-and-log download. Under Settings → Debug recording, Saved sessions offers separate Audio and Telemetry downloads, plus per-session Delete. Delete my recordings removes all this browser’s local debug sessions and requests deletion of its server sessions, including those no longer stored locally. Recording turns off. Offline or uncertain deletion stays pending until the server confirms; the page reports removal only after confirmation. Removing a recording ends all access to it at once; storage backup copies may persist.

Keep this browser’s site data until a pending deletion completes: clearing it loses the device token used to authorize deletion. Access to those recordings still expires after 30 days. Anyone using this browser profile can access its local recordings. Local unkept debug copies expire after 14 days or the 100 MB unkept cap, oldest first; Keep prevents local automatic deletion. Audio capture is capped at 10 minutes and 20 MB per session. These local rules do not extend server retention.

Personal Saves are separate from beta debug sessions and are not uploaded by the debug uploader.
