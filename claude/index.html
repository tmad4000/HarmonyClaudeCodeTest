Harmony Singer

Sing a melody. Hear in-key harmonies sung back with your own voice, live.

Starting saves microphone and harmony audio plus diagnostic events on this browser only. No uploads. Turn off before starting to skip recording. Unkept takes expire after 14 days when this app opens; maximum 100 MB unkept, 10 minutes of audio per session. Kept takes are never automatically deleted. Anyone using this browser profile can review its recordings.

Speakers

The harmony echo canceller subtracts the app’s own sound from the microphone instead of turning the microphone down, so harmonies should stay up while you sing. Only used when listening on speakers.

Mix

Voice engine

Stretch keeps your vowel colour when shifting; granular is lighter on CPU. Piano samples load from tonejs.github.io on first use.

Record and debug

Recordings download as .webm when stopped. The log is JSON: notes, shifts, key, gate, echo-canceller readings and 2 Hz input stats. If speakers misbehave, record a take and save the log.

How it works

  • Press Start singing and allow the microphone. Hold a note; the harmonies follow.
  • Thirds suits most pop melodies; fifths and octaves are bolder; the choir styles add four voices.
  • Leave Root on Auto-detect or pick the song’s key so harmonies stay in key. Chromatic gives fixed parallel intervals.
  • Headphones give the cleanest result. Speakers work too: keep the volume moderate and sing towards the laptop.
