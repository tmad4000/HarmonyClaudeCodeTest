How to Use

  • Click "Start Singing" and allow microphone access
  • Sing into your microphone - the app will detect your pitch
  • Choose a harmony mode: Unison doubles your voice, Thirds for pop, Fifths for folk
  • Select harmony position: Above, Below, or Both for full harmonies
  • Adjust the harmony volume to blend with your voice
  • Use reverb to add space and polish to the sound
  • Pick the song's key (or leave Root on Auto-detect) so harmonies stay in key; Chromatic gives fixed parallel intervals
  • Try different scales - Major sounds happy, Minor sounds emotional
