What is happening?

Musical memory. Twelve recurrent units respond to contour, intensity, onsets and pauses. The coefficients are hand-authored, not trained on songs. Harmony choices are constrained to a fourth, fifth or octave below. The sound is a simple synthesizer, not a cloned singing voice.

Timing. Pitch tracking uses a trailing 40 ms window. A short contour prediction can reduce tracking lag; it cannot know your next note. Rhythmic confidence falls in free time. There is no brain sensing or pitch-cycle phase-lock claim.

Speaker limits. A measured playback path is subtracted from the mic. A conservative guard may mute sustained unison because your voice and returned playback can be indistinguishable. Moving-room and real-singing performance still need listening acceptance.
