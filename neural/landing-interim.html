Open private WAV upload ↗

Use a mono 16-bit PCM WAV at 44.1 kHz, 0.5–10 seconds, up to 900,000 bytes. Rendering can take 20–90 seconds. The test estimates a major key; you can choose the key yourself.

Inference runs on Jacob’s private host. Audio is processed in memory and is not saved by the service. This interim test accepts WAV files. Public HTTPS recording is being coordinated separately.

Waveform generator: OpenVPI PC-NSF-HiFiGAN 2025.02, unchanged weights under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Model release and attribution. Key detection and harmony selection use DSP/rules. Physical Mac/iOS behavior and musical quality still need listening review.
